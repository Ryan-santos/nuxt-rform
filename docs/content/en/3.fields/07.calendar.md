title: Calendar

description: The same calendar RDate opens in a popover, now always visible.
---

`RCalendar` accepts the same `mode`, `time` and `disable` as `RDate` — the
difference is that it does not open in a popover, it is always on screen.

::callout{type="tip"}
`Calendar` exists as a **field** and as a **util** (`RUtilsCalendar`, the panel
`RDate` mounts inside the popover). They do not collide: the translation prefix
is per scope, so one lives under `rform.fields.calendar.*` and the other under
`rform.utils.calendar.*`.
::
