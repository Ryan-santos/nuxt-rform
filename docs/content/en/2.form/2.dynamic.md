`RDynamic` walks a schema and resolves each `type` through the generated
`components-map`. `object` and `array` recurse on their own.

Only `useRForm`'s **Mode 1** feeds `RDynamic`: without a `schema` there is
nothing to render.

`RDynamic` is also what resolves the schema conditionals: see
[Conditionals](/en/form/condicionais).

## slot instead of type

A schema field that declares `slot` instead of `type` hands control back to your
template, with `fieldName` and `rule` in the slot scope.
